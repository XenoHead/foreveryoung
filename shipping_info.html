DHL EXPRESS - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - Forever Young Records ships international orders exclusively DHL Express Direct DDP. DHL Express is one of the world's fastest, most reliable, Carrier Service levels available. 1A) DDP (Duties Taxes Paid) - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -- - - - - - - - 1A.1) Direct DDP means Duties, Taxes, Customs, & Import Fees paid for, and handled by us, the Seller, in advance of a parcel arriving in your country. This results in an item leaving Texas, USA, and arriving at a Buyer's doorstep without hassle! 1A.2) ALL VAT, Duties, Import, and Brokerage Fees, levied by another country's government, or by/through DHL, are handled in advance by Forever Young Records. 1A.3) Under certain circumstances, a citizen of another country may be required to provide necessary ID &/or Tax documentation before an item(s) is/are delivered/released, though the Buyer should never receive a surprise Import/Duty charge! 1A.4) Buyers are encouraged to research their country's Import/Tax laws to ensure proper documentation is provided to Customs Authorities if requested. 2A) DAP (Duties Taxes Unpaid) - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -- - - - - - - - - 2A.1) DAP means Duties, Taxes, Customs, & Import Fees Unpaid (including/excluding VAT depending on Marketplace Facilitator status). 2A.2) Under certain circumstances, an international Buyer may have an order shipped DAP, resulting in that Buyer being responsible for navigating: 2A.2.a) Customs regulations 2A.2.b) Import fee collections levied by the Customs Office of your country, which may include Customs Charges, VAT Taxes, Duties, etc… 2A.2.c) All legally binding Fees that your country assesses upon arrival. 2A.2.d) Carrier Service communication upon arrival to your country. 2A.2.e) Necessary documents required for parcel securement (Ex: Passport, Tax ID, etc...) 2A.2.f) Assessing costs associated with the value of the imported product(s) may include (among others): > Product category and price > Shipping costs and package weight > Customs clearance channel 2A.3) A Carrier Service may also hold an item for a period of time pending a necessary Import / Tax Duty payment from a Buyer/Citizen in the recipient's country. After some time, if the Buyer/Recipient does not pay a country's required Import Fees, the parcel may be returned to the country of origin, and all shipping costs WILL NOT be refunded. - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - B. VAT/GST & IOSS MARKETPLACE FACILITATION - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - VAT stands for "Value Added Tax". For many countries, it is standard to apply VAT ad valorem (as a percentage) to the price of the product being purchased, cost of shipping, and insurance if applicable. 1B) VAT COUNTRIES - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - 1B.1) Some countries have mandatory laws requiring Marketplace Facilitators, such as Discogs, to calculate and charge VAT in advance at the point of checkout. This will appear as an Order Page subtotal line item. 1B.2) Example: European Union Member States, Australia, New Zealand & United Kingdom 2B) VAT ID & IOSS COMPLIANCE - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - 2B.1) Since Forever Young Records ships the majority of product DDP, a Buyer shouldn't have to worry about IOSS and VAT ID shipment inclusion. It is standard operating procedure for Sellers and/or Marketplace Facilitators to include IOSS / VAT ID information with commercial declaration paperwork,as well as, if applicable, electronic declaration information communicated to the Carrier Service. 2B.2) Under select circumstances, if an item is shipped DAP, and VAT was already collected but not remitted to the country in question, a Buyer may qualify for a VAT refund on the unremitted tax through Marketplace Facilitator. Further steps necessary in this circumstance may be found by messaging the Seller, or contacting Discogs Support, for further assistance. - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - C.
